# Generation AI Awards — site

Static site (HTML/CSS/JS, no framework) for the Generation AI Awards premium, rebuilt from the Tilda site https://awards.generation-ai.ru. Content is in Russian. The owner talks to Claude in English.

## Structure

- `index.html` — main page. Hand-edited, self-contained (its own `<style>` and `<script>`). NOT generated.
- All other `*.html` — generated by `_source/build.py`. Do not edit them by hand; edit `_source/data/*` or `build.py` and rebuild.
- `assets/site.css`, `assets/site.js` — shared styles and behaviour of the inner pages.
- `assets/logo.svg` — header logo (all pages, including index.html).
- `statuettes-2026.jpg` — media used by index.html.
- Video on index.html loads from https://just-ai.com/video/GEN_AI_AWARDS_26_1.mp4 (hero `#heroVid` and `#vvid`).

## Build

```
cd _source
pip install markdown
python3 build.py
```

Rebuild after any change to `_source/`, and also after changing the footer in `index.html`: the inner pages copy the main page's footer at build time (`main_footer()`), rewriting `#about`/`#nom` to `index.html#about`/`index.html#nom`.

## Content sources (`_source/data/`)

- `shortlist_2026.txt`, `shortlist_2025.txt` — `## Nomination`, `W: logo | Company | Project` (winner), `F: logo | Company | Project` (finalist). Logo is a local path (`images/logos/*.webp`) or `-` for none.
- `jury.txt` — `J: Name | Role`. Photos are mapped in `JURY_PH` in build.py.
- `noms.py` — nominations: slug, title, description, criteria. Also feeds section 5 of protocol.html via `[[CRITERIA]]`.
- `ai_navigator.md`, `analytical_review.md`, `protocol.md`, `ink.md`, `choice.md` — Markdown plus site blocks:
  - `:::kind` … `+++` (next card, optional modifier e.g. `+++ dont`) … `:::` → card grid. Kinds: intro, qa, callouts, steps, versus, dodont, experts, icons, template, quote, test, trends, duo, advice (jury photo + name/role + advice on a grey-to-black gradient, AI-Навигатор), note (one full-width lime card, AI-Навигатор «Важно!»), split (two cards side by side, each with its own `## heading` inside; a `!figure` line in a card puts a photo + the quote after it below the text; `+++ bright` makes a card the lime plate — Инк «Какой приз?» + «Как принять участие?») — styled in site.css as `.blk-<kind>`.
  - Card first line may be `!icon URL`, `!person Name | Role`, `!photo URL | Name | Role`, `!figure URL | alt` (photo + quote inside a `split` card), `!juror URL | Name | Role` (large jury photo with name and role, used by `advice`).
  - `C: Company | type | task | solution | result | nomination` → case card (only in `analytical_review.md`). Its logo comes from `case_logos.txt` (`Company | images/cases/*.webp`, round 144×144 images shown as 64px circles); a company without one gets a same-size circle with its initials. МТС shows the VisionLabs logo (the case is VisionLabs technology), at the owner's request.
  - `[[CTA text | button | href]]`, `[[META a | b]]`, `[[COVER url]]`, `[[TOC]]`.
- `gal2026.txt`, `gallery_2025.txt` — gallery photo paths, one per line, local WebP files in `images/gallery-<year>/` (2026 page also shows `statuettes-2026.jpg` first).

## Conventions and decisions already made

- Design: dark background, lime accent `#c6f766`, violet `#2720a7`, Montserrat. All H2 are 40px max (`clamp(28px,3.2vw,40px)`).
- Layout: all content sits in a 1440px field (`--field`; `.wrap` is `min(1440px, 100% - 2*pad)`; full-bleed rails use `--fpad` to line up with it). Every block has `padding: 180px 0` (`--sec`, 110px under 760px). The video block is the one full-bleed exception.
- Buttons have no arrows. Carousel ←/→ controls stay.
- Jury photos are local: `assets/jury/<surname>.webp` (4:3, colour, no grayscale). Map in `JURY_PH` (build.py) and `JURY` (index.html).
- Index nominations: steps 1–2 stacked in one column beside the AI-Навигатор card, both columns equal width (laid out like the review card `.rbox`, own violet fill); 12 cards in a 4-column grid, no numbers; the «Инк» special prize is a full-width plate in brand violet `#2720a7` with a gradient.
- Button labels must sit in a `<span>` inside `.btn`, otherwise the white hover fill covers the text. On card-links (`.nav2`, `.nspecial`) hovering the card fills the button too.
- Gallery → winners gap is a single 180px (`#gallery + #winners .bcta{padding-top:0}`).
- Organizer block: H2 «Организатор премии», horizontal Just AI logo `assets/justai.svg` centred on a plate styled like the partner/participant cells (`.oplate`), plate and text top-aligned.
- Index gallery: equal-height photos, natural width, endless loop (two identical sets).
- Gallery pages show the ceremony video above the photos (`GAL_VIDEO` in build.py).
- Nomination pages (`body.nom-page`): criteria as cards, apply + help plates in one row, 180px between blocks. Text nominations (`nom-text`: Инк, Выбор генеративного AI) keep the intro paragraph next to the lead; their H2 sections are 180px apart. Each also has `nom-<slug>`; on `nom-ink` the card blocks span the whole 1440px field (headings and plain paragraphs stay 860px), theses 4 per row, prize and how-to side by side in one `split` block (prize card on the navigator's grey-to-black gradient).
- «От чат-бота к агенту» and «AI-Навигатор 2.0» (`article(..., side_toc=True)`, both sources have `[[TOC]]` after `[[COVER]]`): two columns — the article 970px on the left, the `[[TOC]]` moved into a sticky right column (`.art-side`) whose current section turns lime (site.js); article H3 28px with 90px top margin; case cards 2 per row, `dt` 15px; the test block (`:::test`) is one horizontal card per question — number, question, verdict. One column under 1100px with the contents on top. Navigator specifics: intro is plain paragraphs; «Проекты с низкими баллами» list uses coral minus signs; in `:::dodont` card titles are `####` (h4, margin-top 16px, p margin-top 8px) and «Не стоит» cards sit in the left column, «Приветствуется» in the right (CSS `grid-column`, source order unchanged — a block's first card can't carry a modifier).
- Header: logo image; the current page's link is lime (`aria-current`, `.dd.on` on inner pages; scroll-spy `.on` for #about/#nom on index.html).
- No custom cursor, no variants panel (both removed on purpose — don't bring back).
- index.html opens at the top: the timeline tabs scroll only their own strip, never the page.
- Nominations on index.html are cards that are plain links to `nomination-*.html`.
- Nomination pages have no forms. "Заполнить заявку" links to the live form: `APPLY_BASE` in build.py (`https://awards.generation-ai.ru/nomination/<slug>`). Note the live "Выбор генеративного AI" URL has a typo, `/nomintaion/opensource`; it is correct as is.
- External on purpose: Кейсориум (generation-ai.ru), the "Что думают члены жюри…" article, privacy policy PDF, Telegram links.
- Partner logos on index.html are local SVGs in `assets/partners/`, listed with their links in `PARTNERS` (order, name, URL); each cell is a link opening in a new tab, no numbers.
- Participant logos on index.html are local SVGs in `assets/participants/`, listed in `LOGOS` (file, name); same hairline table as the partners, 6 per row (4 under 900px, 2 under 480px); the free cells of the last row of six merge into one `.fill` cell with «здесь может быть ваша компания» in Caveat.
- No Tilda dependency: every image is a local file. Winner/finalist and review logos live in `images/logos/` (WebP, trimmed to the logo on white), article images in `images/articles/`. Never hotlink tildacdn.com again.
